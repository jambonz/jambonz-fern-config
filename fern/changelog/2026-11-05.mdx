This entry adds Saidwire as a speech-to-text vendor for English.

#### New Features & Improvements

1. **Saidwire speech-to-text** — `saidwire` is available as a realtime STT vendor, with
   speech credentials in the portal and API. Saidwire follows Deepgram's streaming
   protocol and is handled like `deepgram`: interim results, a final transcript per
   utterance, and jambonz's own turn detection in the [agent](/verbs/verbs/agent) verb. It is
   English only and mono, and reports a fixed confidence of 0.95. `saidwireOptions` sets
   `punctuate`, `smartFormatting`, `endpointing`, `utteranceEndMs` and `vadEvents`. See
   [recognizer](/verbs/verbs/recognizer).
