jambonz Commercial 11.1.11 (jambonz.cloud release)
</Card>

**11.1.11** adds [AssemblyAI](/guides/features/tts-vendor-settings/assemblyai) as a text-to-speech vendor and ships
[Paxa Labs](/guides/features/tts-vendor-settings/paxa) text-to-speech and speech-to-text. The [agent](/verbs/verbs/agent) verb can open a call with
exact words instead of an LLM-generated greeting, and its `turn_end` event now covers the
greeting turn and says when each side began speaking. Barge-in fades the agent's audio out
instead of cutting it off with a click. The release also sends webhooks over HTTP/1.1 again,
backs off from webhook URLs that keep answering 404, and reports why a streaming TTS vendor
refused the text.

#### New Features & Improvements

1. **AssemblyAI text-to-speech** — `assemblyai`, already a speech-to-text vendor, is now
   available for text-to-speech too, with streaming TTS, word-level alignment and regional
   endpoints set by the credential. See [AssemblyAI](/guides/features/tts-vendor-settings/assemblyai).
1. **Paxa Labs** — text-to-speech and realtime speech-to-text for Thai, English and Mandarin
   Chinese from one `paxa` credential, as described in the October 28 entry. See
   [Paxa Labs](/guides/features/tts-vendor-settings/paxa).
1. **Agent greeting as text** — the [agent](/verbs/verbs/agent) verb's `greeting` may now be a string: the
   agent speaks those exact words as its first turn, with no LLM call. The caller can
   interrupt it, and the history records it as the agent's opening, as with a generated
   greeting. `true` and `false` work as before.
1. **Agent `turn_end` timing** — `turn_end` is now sent for the greeting turn too
   (`greeting: true`, empty `transcript`), so a listener sees every turn the agent spoke. Each
   `turn_end` also carries `caller_started_at` and `agent_started_at` (epoch ms), so a turn
   that ends late (for example one held open by a transfer) can be placed where it happened.
1. **Smoother barge-in** — when the caller interrupts, the agent's audio fades out over 60 ms
   instead of stopping between two frames, which callers heard as a click. This applies to
   streaming TTS, speech-to-speech, Dialogflow CES streaming, audio playout from a
   [listen](/verbs/verbs/listen) stream, and stopping `play` or `say`.
1. **Deepgram Multilingual** — a Deepgram speech credential now lists the Multilingual
   (`multi`) language for the nova-2 and nova-3 models in the portal and API. Deepgram's
   model list never includes it, although both models accept it.

#### Bug Fixes

- **Webhooks over HTTP/1.1** — since the HTTP client upgrade in an earlier release, HTTPS
  webhooks (for example anything behind Cloudflare) were sent over HTTP/2, where a request
  could sit on a dead connection until it failed with `Headers Timeout Error`. Webhook
  requests use HTTP/1.1 again. WebSocket applications and vendor traffic are unaffected.
- **Webhook URLs that keep answering 404** — after a URL answers 404 three times in a row,
  requests to it fail at once with the same 404 for the next 60 seconds instead of being
  sent; any other answer resets the count. Skipped requests are logged once per account per
  minute and do not raise alerts. Timeouts and 5xx responses are handled as before.
- **Streaming TTS refusals** — when a streaming TTS vendor refused the text mid-stream (for
  example Rime with "Invalid speaker"), the agent went silent and nothing said why. The
  vendor's reason is now logged, raised as a `TTS_FAILURE` alert, and sent to the agent's
  eventHook as `tts_error`.
- **Barge-in during long replies** — a barge-in during a long Dialogflow CES or
  speech-to-speech reply could wait until the queued audio had played out, and a barge-in
  during streaming TTS could let the rest of a cancelled reply play. Both now stop at once.
- **ElevenLabs agents** — an ElevenLabs agent that speaks at a different rate from the one
  it listens at (for example `pcm_16000` in, `pcm_24000` out) played slow and low-pitched. Its
  audio now plays at the agent's own output rate.
- **OpenAI Realtime tool results** — a tool result sent back as a plain `{result}` or `{error}`
  object, rather than a `conversation.item.create`, was dropped, leaving the caller in
  silence. It is now sent to the model as the function call's output.
- **Refused websocket applications** — when the verbs a websocket app returned for
  `session:new` failed validation, the call was declined and the socket closed with no
  reason. jambonz now sends a `jambonz:error` message with the validation error before
  closing.
- **Out-of-dialog REFER** — drachtio 10.1.12 passes an out-of-dialog REFER (RFC 7647) to
  the application instead of discarding it, and no longer leaks a dialog entry for each
  subscription that ends with a terminating NOTIFY.
- **Security** — the HTTP client library (undici) is updated to 8.12.3 for its recent
  security releases.
