title: jambonz.cloud Interoperability Specification
---

This document outlines the SIP and RTP Interoperability details of the jambonz.cloud hosted system.

# SIP specification

The SIP signaling address(es) for jambonz.cloud are shown below.  You should make sure your firewall allows traffic from and to these addresses.
- 54.236.168.131

The following protocols are supported:
- UDP, port 5060
- TCP, port 5060
- TLS, port 5061
- SIP over websockets (webrtc clients), port 8443

As part of your jambonz.cloud account you have a dedicated SIP FQDN; e.g. something along the lines of "mysubdomain.jambonz.cloud". 
You can have your carriers send to that FQDN or to the static IP address directly.  If you are registering a SIP client device to 
jambonz.cloud then you must REGISTER with your FQDN.

# RTP specification

The RTP media address(es) for jambonz.cloud are shown below.  You should make sure your firewall allows traffic from and to these addresses.
- 54.144.249.44
- 3.218.246.146

The RTP port range on the jambonz.cloud side is 40000-60000.

The following codecs are supported:
- PCMU
- PCMA
- G722
- OPUS
