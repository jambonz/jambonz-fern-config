---
title: Realtime voice API
subtitle: One vendor's model listens, thinks and speaks. jambonz carries the call, adds the transfer and hangup tools, and records every turn.
---

A **Realtime voice API** agent hands the whole voice loop to one vendor: speech recognition, turn-taking, barge-in,
the model and the voice all run inside the vendor's session. You choose the vendor, the model and the voice. It
gives the lowest latency and the most natural turn-taking, with one vendor to configure. jambonz runs it with the
[llm verb](/verbs/verbs/llm).

You write the prompt and add tools and knowledge in Studio, as for a pipeline agent. The vendor, model and voice are
in the agent's **Model & voice** section.

## Vendors and credentials

Each vendor needs its own credential in your jambonz account, added in the jambonz portal under **LLM Services**,
on the **Speech-to-speech** tab. It is a credential of its own, separate from any language model or speech
credential for the same vendor.

| Vendor | Starting model | Starting voice | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI Realtime** | `gpt-realtime` | `marin` | Also suggests `gpt-realtime-mini`. |
| **OpenAI GPT-Live** | `gpt-live-1` | `marin` | Tool calls, transfers and hangups run on a separate **Model for delegated work** (starts on `gpt-6.1-terra`). |
| **Rime Prism** | none | `astra` | No model choice. Four voices: `astra`, `aurora`, `brando` and `wawona`. English only. |
| **Deepgram Voice Agent** | `gpt-4.1-mini` | `flux-kit-en` | Deepgram listens (Flux for English, Nova-3 otherwise), runs the LLM and speaks with a Flux or Aura voice, and bills all three to your Deepgram account. |
| **Ultravox** | `ultravox-v0.7` | `Olivia` | Ultravox has many voices; Studio suggests a few English ones. |

You choose the vendor in the **New agent** dialog. If the account has no credential for it yet, you can still build
the agent and add the credential later.

## Model & voice

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | One of the five vendors above. Switching vendor clears the credential and starts from that vendor's model and voice. |
| **Credential** | The vendor's credential from your jambonz account. When you have more than one, the label picks which. |
| **Model** | The vendor's model. For Deepgram Voice Agent this is **LLM (managed by Deepgram)**. Not shown for Rime Prism. |
| **Voice** | The vendor's voice. |
| **Language** | The language of the conversation (`en-US` to start). |
| **Model for delegated work** | OpenAI GPT-Live only: the model its tool calls, transfers and hangups run on. |

For every field you can pick a suggestion or type any model, voice or language code the vendor accepts.

### Options

The **Options** fold takes extra vendor settings as JSON:

- **OpenAI Realtime** and **OpenAI GPT-Live**: any key from the OpenAI section of the [llm verb](/verbs/verbs/llm)
  docs, for example `turn_detection` or `temperature`. Studio asks OpenAI Realtime to transcribe the caller, so the
  call history has the caller's words; set `input_audio_transcription` here to change that.
- **Rime Prism**: only `turn_detection` (`interrupt_response`, `eagerness`, `prism_end_of_turn_timeout_ms`) and
  `truncation`. Anything else is left out.
- **Deepgram Voice Agent**: `listen`, `think` and `speak` are merged into Deepgram's providers; `mip_opt_out`, `tags`
  and `flags` go in the settings.
- **Ultravox**: any setting of an Ultravox call, such as `temperature`, `vadSettings` or `inactivityMessages`.

Do not put the prompt, tools or voice here; Studio adds them. For Rime Prism, Deepgram Voice Agent and Ultravox,
Studio notes any option it leaves out.

### Handled by the vendor

Speech recognition, turn detection, barge-in, the voice and noise handling happen inside the vendor's session. The
pipeline's turn-taking and interruption settings do not appear in the **Conversation** section for these agents;
tune them through the vendor's options.

### Greeting

An **OpenAI Realtime** agent always speaks first. If its greeting is set to wait for the caller, Studio notes that it
will not wait. Rime Prism and OpenAI GPT-Live can wait for the caller.

## Studio's own lines

On a warm transfer, Studio speaks a brief to the person who answers ("Brief the person first", in **Transfer &
hangup**). The vendor's voice is inside its session, so Studio speaks the brief itself, with a text-to-speech
credential from your account. The secure detour's message is different: a Realtime voice API agent says that line
itself, in its own voice.

When the agent has a warm transfer with a brief, **Model & voice** shows **Voice for Studio's own lines**:

- **Speak them in the agent's voice**: shown when the vendor also sells the agent's voice as text-to-speech. Turn it
  on and Studio speaks the brief in the same voice as the agent:

  | Vendor | When it is offered | Text-to-speech credential needed |
  |---|---|---|
  | Deepgram Voice Agent | a Flux voice (`flux-…`) or an Aura voice (`aura-…`) | Deepgram Flux for a Flux voice, Deepgram for an Aura voice |
  | Rime Prism | the `astra` or `wawona` voice | RimeLabs |

  Studio offers only credentials of that vendor, and uses the agent's voice. If the chosen credential is from another
  vendor, Studio notes that it needs one from the agent's vendor.
- Otherwise, choose a text-to-speech credential and a voice for Studio's lines. OpenAI Realtime, OpenAI GPT-Live and
  Ultravox voices are not sold as text-to-speech, so for those the brief is spoken in a voice you choose here.

If no voice is chosen, Studio notes that it has no voice to speak the brief with. The transfer's **Brief for the
person** field in **Transfer & hangup** shows which voice will speak it, with a link back here.
