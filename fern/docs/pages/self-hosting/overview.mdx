# Self-Hosting jambonz

This section provides comprehensive guides for deploying and managing jambonz on your own infrastructure.

## What is Self-Hosting?

Self-hosting jambonz gives you complete control over your telephony infrastructure, allowing you to:

- Deploy on your own cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Azure, etc.)
- Run on-premises in your data center
- Customize and extend the platform to meet specific requirements
- Maintain complete data sovereignty and privacy
- Scale according to your specific needs

## Getting Started

Before you begin self-hosting jambonz, you should be familiar with:

- Container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
- SIP telephony basics
- Cloud infrastructure management
- Database administration (MySQL/MariaDB, Redis)
